N5 Life Worker App – Safety Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

N5 Life is committed to maintaining a safe and trustworthy environment for customers and service professionals. This Safety Policy explains the safety expectations for workers using the N5 Life Worker App.

1. Worker Identity

Workers must use their own approved N5 Life account when providing services. Workers must not allow another person to use their account or represent them during a customer service request.

2. Verification

N5 Life may verify worker identity and professional information using documents or information submitted during registration.

Verification is intended to support trust on the platform and does not guarantee a worker's conduct, qualifications, service quality or future behaviour.

3. Safe Customer Interaction

Workers must behave respectfully and professionally when communicating with customers. Threatening, abusive, intimidating or inappropriate behaviour is not permitted.

4. Home Service Safety

Workers visiting a customer's home or property should follow reasonable safety practices and respect the customer's property.

Workers should only perform work that they are suitably experienced, qualified or licensed to perform where applicable.

5. Tools and Equipment

Workers are responsible for using appropriate tools and equipment safely and for following applicable safety requirements when performing their services.

6. No Unsafe or Illegal Work

Workers must not knowingly perform illegal, dangerous or prohibited activities through the N5 Life platform.

7. Customer Privacy

Workers may receive customer information necessary to fulfil a service request. Such information must be used only for legitimate service purposes and must not be misused or shared without permission or a lawful reason.

8. Suspicious or Unsafe Situations

If a worker encounters a dangerous, threatening or suspicious situation, the worker should prioritize personal safety, leave the situation when reasonably necessary and contact appropriate emergency services when required.

9. Reporting Safety Concerns

Workers should report serious safety concerns, threats, fraud, harassment or other dangerous behaviour related to the platform to N5 Life.

10. Emergency Situations

N5 Life is not an emergency service. In an immediate emergency or situation involving danger to life or property, workers should contact the appropriate local emergency services immediately.

11. Safety Violations

N5 Life may investigate reported safety issues and may restrict, suspend or terminate accounts where there is a violation of this Safety Policy, the Terms of Service or applicable law.
